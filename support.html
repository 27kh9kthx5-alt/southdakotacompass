Frequently asked questions

Where is my data stored?

Everything you enter stays on your device and syncs through your own private iCloud account. It isn't sent to us or to any central server, and it's never sold or used for ads.

How do I sync across devices?

Sign every device into the same iCloud account with iCloud Drive enabled. Your students, observations, and settings will sync automatically. Give it a minute and make sure each device has an internet connection.

My data isn't showing up on another device.

Check three things: the devices are signed into the same Apple ID, iCloud is turned on in the device Settings, and both devices are online. Sync can take a moment after adding new records.

How do I add or transfer students and centers?

Use the add buttons on the roster and setup screens. When adding a center, you can tap a suggested center to add it instantly, and tap it again to remove it if you change your mind.

How do I generate a report?

Open a student or a report view and choose the report you want. You can export it as a PDF and share it using the standard iOS share sheet.

How do I delete my data?

You can delete individual students, observations, or other records from within the app. To remove everything, delete the app and clear its data from your iCloud storage in device Settings.
